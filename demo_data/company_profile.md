# AIA Studio Demo Company Profile

AIA Studio helps small and medium businesses launch practical digital systems.

## Services

AIA Studio provides:
- professional business websites
- AI-assisted customer support
- lead capture and follow-up automation
- internal knowledge assistants
- repetitive workflow automation
- system deployment and maintenance

## Target Customers

The primary customers are small and medium businesses that want practical automation without building a large internal technical team.

## Support

Standard support is available during business hours. Critical production incidents are prioritized according to service agreements.

## Data Handling

Customer documents are used only to provide the requested knowledge and automation services. Access should be limited to authorized users.
